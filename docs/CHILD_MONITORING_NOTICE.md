# A note for the person using this computer

GuardianNode is running on this computer. Your parent or guardian set it up to
help notice online situations that may need a conversation or support.

## What it can see

GuardianNode may take pictures of what is visible on the screen and read visible
words. It may record the app name, window title, time, and website address as
context. It looks for patterns connected with things such as bullying, scams,
sexual pressure, grooming, and self-harm.

It does not secretly record keystrokes or turn on the camera or microphone. The
GuardianNode shield remains visible. Monitoring can make mistakes: it can miss
something important or flag something harmless.

## Where the information goes

By default, analysis and storage happen on your family's own GuardianNode
server. A parent can optionally ask an outside AI service for a second opinion,
but GuardianNode shows exactly what would be sent and requires a separate parent
choice each time. That service can also make mistakes.

## Your choices and questions

You can ask your parent or guardian:

- What is being monitored and why.
- To look at an alert together and add context or correct it.
- How long information is kept.
- To pause monitoring for a private or parent-owned session.
- To delete information or stop monitoring when it is no longer appropriate.
- To review a request you submit through GuardianNode.

GuardianNode should support a family conversation, not replace trust or be used
as secret surveillance or automatic punishment. An alert is not proof that
anyone did something wrong.

If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact a trusted adult or
local emergency services. In the United States and its territories, call or
text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. GuardianNode is not an emergency
service.

Parents: discuss this notice in language appropriate for the child's age before
turning on monitoring, and revisit it when the child, device, monitoring scope,
retention, or external-AI choice changes.
